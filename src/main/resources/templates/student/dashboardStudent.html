How to Request Leftover Food

Step 1: Navigate to "View Leftover" Find the 'View Leftover' menu option.
Find the "View Leftover" option in the sidebar menu to see the available leftover food.
Step 2: Request Food Click on the 'Request' button.
Hover over the Action column where the "Request" button will appear. Click on it to make a request.
Step 3: Wait for Notification Wait for notification approval.
Once your request is approved by the cafeteria, you will receive a notification. Please wait patiently.
Step 4: Pickup the Leftover Confirm and pick up leftover food.
Go to the "Pickup Leftover" menu and fill out the required form to confirm your pickup. Submit the form to complete the process.
